Before you begin — read carefully
  • The exam opens in fullscreen. Leaving fullscreen submits your exam.
  • Switching tabs, minimizing, or clicking outside the window submits your exam.
  • On a phone, an incoming call or a notification you tap counts as leaving the exam. Turn on Do Not Disturb before you start.
  • Copy, paste, right-click and developer tools are disabled.
  • You get one attempt only. Contact your invigilator if you are locked out.
